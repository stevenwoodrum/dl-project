This approach combines frozen MiniLM SBERT embeddings of the task text with hashed model metadata and four simple text statistics. These features feed an FNN with 32 and 16 ReLU hidden neurons and a sigmoid output that predicts the probability of a correct answer. Training uses Adam and Brier loss, with equal total weight per model-benchmark pair, dropout, and L2 regularization. Development uses prompt-grouped validation, early stopping, learning-rate scheduling, and leave-one-benchmark-out evaluation. The final FNN is then trained from scratch on all eligible binary training data.
